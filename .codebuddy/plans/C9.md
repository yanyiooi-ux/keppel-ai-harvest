# C9: AOM chat, impact meter and charge decision [step C9 done]

Built by Claude Code (not CodeBuddy), after CodeBuddy's credit ran out. The AOM, Tower A (Keppel Asset Operations
Manager, role-played) is the only seat that decides a charge (A4). Code works out every number on this seat by
replaying the tickets, and code writes the tenant's card after the decision. The AI writes, suggests and sees no
amount, and no charge step makes an AI call.

## Who uses it, what decision it serves, the look, the phases
- Who: the AOM, Tower A (role-played), and judges watching the demo.
- Decision it serves: who pays for work on the tenant's fit-out (Tenant pays with an amount, Landlord pays, or
  Goodwill, no charge), and how much the repeat calls at Tower A Level 23 cost before Pill Desk and with it.
- Look and style options: already locked by YY on 14 Oct 2026, Neon Quest, the tokens in web/app/globals.css :root
  only; no new colour, a status colour always carries its word. No style options to offer: the look is decided.
- Phases: (1) tests first, (2) the meter, (3) the press, (4) the AOM desk view, (5) the screen, (6) the standard
  ending, (7) the C9 selfcheck (rule 3f), (8) the proof row.

## Items, each failing test first

1. **`web/lib/impact.ts`: `impactFor(spotKey, allTickets, rates)`**, exactly as S18, by replaying the tickets.
   - The run of calls: the spot's tickets in time order, each within 30 days of the one before. When a spot has more
     than one run, the meter shows the newest one, the run "and counting" is about.
   - Before: all calls in the run; one visit per call; 0 senior checks; "not fixed after {first to last call} days";
     calls minus 1 repeat calls; visits x visit rate, "and counting".
   - With Pill Desk: calls up to and including the first one where `computeRepeatFlag` (the clock rule, the job's
     report time plus 20 minutes, `repeatFlagFor` in facts.ts) is on; one visit per call up to it; 1 senior check, on
     that visit; days from the first call to the caught call; the caught call's position minus 1 repeat calls;
     visits x visit rate + senior checks x senior rate. When no call in the run trips the flag, nothing is caught and
     the With Pill Desk side says so.
   - Days are whole calendar days in Singapore time.
   - Tower A Level 23: before 5 calls, 5 visits, 0 senior checks, not fixed after 21 days, 4 repeat calls, S$600 and
     counting; with Pill Desk caught at 2305 on 19 Sep, 2 calls, 2 visits, 1 senior check, 4 days, 1 repeat call,
     S$420. Nothing typed in, nothing hard-coded.
   - The label, always: "Worked out on made-up demo data. It assumes the cause is found on the visit where the repeat
     is caught."
2. **A test changes a date in a temp copy of `tickets.json`** (outside the repo data folder) and the numbers change; a
   changed rate in a temp copy of `impact-rates.json` changes the cost.
3. **`web/lib/aom-desk.ts`** (read only, returned by `seatView` as the new `aom` field): the meter for Tower A Level 23
   from `callsForState(state)` and `loadImpactRates()`; the speak-first flag for every Tower A spot whose speak-first
   notice is in the memory within 30 days of the demo clock, with the notice's own words; the charge requests (A4)
   for this seat, open ones first, each with what the AOM decides and, once decided, the decision and the tenant's card
   word for word; the safety alerts sent to this seat and the red stops open in Tower A now; the live version in Tower
   A with its version line (read only, no button); and the Not solved impact records of Tower A jobs with their cost
   from the made-up rates.
4. **`decide-charge`** (A4, cut 9, `web/lib/charge-flow.ts`): the gate in permissions.ts already lets only the AOM
   press it. Refused, with a plain reason and nothing changed, unless: an open A4 request (by its id, else the job's
   open one); a choice of Tenant pays, Landlord pays or Goodwill, no charge; for Tenant pays a whole-number amount from
   1 to 100000 (a number, from the number field); a reason of 3 words or more for every choice; no amount in the
   reason (the amount goes only in the number field). Then code marks the request decided (choice, amount for Tenant
   pays only, reason, who, when) and posts the tenant's card in the tenant chat, word for word: "What we will do:
   {tenantSolution} Cost: {"S${amount}, made-up demo amount" | "no charge to you"}. Approved by the AOM
   (role-played)." Nothing reaches the tenant before. A decided request cannot be decided again. No AI call: the press
   goes through runAction only, and the cards carry "AI calls: 0".
   - The after-hours request (S10) has no tenantSolution in the spec. Built for now: "What we will do:" uses the
     request's own words with a full stop ("After-hours air-con for Northwind Advisory, Level 23."). Question 10 in
     QUESTIONS.md, one it.todo.
5. **What needs me** on the AOM seat drops a charge request once it is decided (the notice names its request by
   `ref`, the same way HQ's notices do since the C8b review). The **Impact** chip (AOM only, S19) answers with the
   meter for the job's spot, else Tower A Level 23, and the job's impact record when it has one, instead of "Open a
   job first": the AOM seat has no job of its own.
6. **The screen** (`web/app/components/chat/AomDesk.tsx` inside SeatChat): the meter card with two tabs, Before and
   With Pill Desk, opening on Before (a view only, client state), each tab showing calls, visits, senior manager
   checks, days to a real fix, repeat calls and cost, and the label under both; the speak-first flag with its word;
   each charge request with its text and, while open, a form: the three choices, a number field (whole number, 1 to
   100000) for Tenant pays, a reason, and Decide charge; a refused press shows its reason inside that request's card;
   the safety alerts; the live version; the impact records. The AOM desk shows the alerts and charge requests itself,
   so the seat's plain notice list leaves those two kinds out on this seat only. Phone first: one column, presses at
   least 44 px tall, no sideways scroll at 390 px.
