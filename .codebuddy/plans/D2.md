# D2: Story mode at /demo

Part D2 of the story mode plan (15 Oct 2026). YY saw the finished app and said it is confusing to show: you hunt
across 5 chats and nothing tells the story. He picked Story mode: one page that plays the real app, one seat at a
time, with a short plain caption for each step. D1 (plain words, loading you can see) is built; D2 reuses it.

## Hard limits (not questions)

- Story mode drives the real app. It presses through POST /api/reset, /api/action and /api/chat and reads
  /api/state, the same routes every seat uses. Nothing is pre-rendered or recorded. One line on screen says so.
- No engine change: no change to engine output, API response shapes, pill text, the safety stop wording (S11),
  tips, or any code reason that feeds a saved AI answer's replay key. web/data/replay/ is never touched.
- The presses are demo-flow's, in demo-flow's order, with demo-flow's payloads (web/tests/demo-flow.test.ts).
  The senior manager types the demo's own words (web/lib/demo-words.ts), so the saved real AI draft shows.
- Every number and claim in a caption is checked by a test against the engine's state right after that step.
  If they differ, the caption changes, never the engine.
- Look: Neon Quest tokens. Every new colour is a token in :root.
- Known limit, said on screen: one shared demo memory, so Story mode's Restart and Back reset what anyone else
  is looking at.

## Build

1. web/lib/story.ts (new, pure, safe in the browser): the 8 steps (seat, short title, caption, presses, what to
   highlight), the runner for one press (it fills in the self-check's job, the open charge request and the
   raised stop from what the app said), how long a step holds (6 to 9 s, longer for longer captions), the
   `?step=` reader, the on-screen lines and the close line.
2. web/app/components/story/StoryPlayer.tsx (new): the player. One seat at a time, big: the real SeatChat of
   the seat that acts, driven by the player (it hands the view in, locks the controls and shows "Pip is
   checking" while each real press runs). After each press the view is read again; a glowing outline marks
   what changed in this step and the page scrolls to it. Controls: Back, Play or Pause, Next, Restart; "Step n
   of 8" with a progress bar; space and the arrow keys. Autoplay holds each step for its time. Back is Reset,
   then a quick replay of the real presses up to the step before. A refused or failed press stops autoplay and
   shows the plain reason; nothing is faked. Controls are disabled while a press is on its way.
3. SeatChat (small, additive): optional `view` (the parent hands the view in and owns every read), `locked`
   (every control off, for Story mode) and `pending` ("Pip is checking" from outside). With none of them set,
   nothing changes.
4. web/app/demo/page.tsx (new route): reads the AI switch and `?step=`. If the switch is not on Saved answers,
   it moves it there once (the cookie) and reloads, and the page says so.
5. Links: home ("Watch the story") and the top of /how. The minutes in the link are measured on a real run.
6. CSS: desktop has the caption panel on the left (sticky) and the seat on the right. At 390 px it is one
   column, with the caption and controls fixed at the bottom and no sideways scroll.

## Tests (failing first)

- web/tests/d2-story.test.ts: the script is demo-flow's presses in demo-flow's order with its payloads; the
  whole story runs through the route code (actionTurn, chatTurn, resetDemo, seatView) on Saved answers with
  the network blocked, and after each step its state and every number in its caption are checked; a press out
  of order comes back refused with the app's reason (never a fake success); captions are short (at most 20
  words a sentence, no unexplained short forms); hold times; `?step=`.
- web/tests/d2-story-ui.test.tsx (fake fetch): autoplay presses in order and holds; Next, Back (Reset, then
  the quick replay), Pause, Restart and the keys; controls are disabled while a press is on its way; a refused
  press stops autoplay with its reason; `?step=n` replays to step n and stops; SeatChat's new props.

## Checks

- `npx vitest run` and `npx next build` pass; demo-flow still prints "demo flow: all steps passed".
- Render on a production build (REPLAY_MODE=1, port 4710): /demo at every step at 1280 and 390, home and /how,
  each PNG looked at; one full autoplay run in Chrome with each step's time on screen written down.
