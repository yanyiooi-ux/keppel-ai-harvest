# D3: the new look, dark glass, and Bolt Pip

Part D3 of the story mode plan (15 Oct 2026). YY saw look samples and changed the look: Neon Quest is out. The new
look is dark glass, matched to YY's reference (a near-black trading dashboard with frosted panels), and Pip becomes
Bolt Pip. This part is visual only.

## Hard limits (not questions)

- Visual only: CSS, fonts, the Pip drawing, and class names only where unavoidable. Engine output, API response
  shapes, pill text, the safety stop wording, tips and every reason string that feeds a saved AI answer's replay key
  stay byte for byte. web/data/replay/ is never touched.
- Every colour is a token in the :root block of web/app/globals.css, hex or rgba (tests/c5a.test.tsx). Nothing
  after :root holds a hex or an rgba.
- A status colour always carries its word.
- Engine and data tests stay byte for byte. An old UI test that pins a Neon Quest value (a token name or hex, the
  Chakra Petch font, Pip's old drawing) may be updated to the new value only, listed old to new in the build log.

## The spec

The picked sample (look-samples/dark-glass-ref.html in the AXION worktree) is the spec for colours, type, panels,
pills and buttons. Bolt Pip is the pip-bolt symbol in look-samples/cute-pip.html.

## Build

1. Tokens. Copy the sample's :root (bg #08090c, panel rgba(22,23,29,.62), panel-2, line rgba(255,255,255,.08),
   line-2, text #ececef, muted #8e909b, faint, violet #8b8cf6, green #4ade80, amber #fbbf24, red #f87171 and their
   tinted backgrounds) into globals.css. Map every existing token name onto the new values, so each component
   re-skins through them:
   - --card, --card-2, --surface and --red-card stay opaque hex: they are the panel's colour as it looks over the
     near-black (the contrast test reads them), while the glass fill itself is the new --panel.
   - --accent becomes the violet; the one main action uses new --main and --main-ink (white, near-black text).
   - --line and --line-2 become the white hairlines; --lit and --link a light violet that clears 4.5:1.
   - --font-head and --font-hud become Geist; --hud-case none, --hud-track 0, --head-weight 500.
   - Status: --g, --a, --r stay mapped to --green, --amber, --red; --g-bg and --a-bg become the green and amber
     tints for pills.
   - The old --pip-* names map onto Bolt Pip's tokens.
2. Background: near-black with a soft violet light in the top-left corner and a faint second light top-right,
   both as gradient tokens. The grid goes.
3. Panels: cards, the seat list, the /how tiles, the live columns and the quest rail are frosted panels
   (--panel, 1px --line border, 22 px radius, a thin top shine). Rows inside a card are --panel-2 with a 14 px
   radius. The top bar and the sticky bars are glass with a blur, opaque enough to read over what scrolls under.
4. Type: Geist from next/font with Inter as the fallback; Chakra Petch removed. Headings weight 500 with tight
   letter spacing; the big page headings get the sample's white-to-grey text fade. Labels (card headers, desk
   sub-heads, kickers, table heads) are sentence case in muted #8e909b; body text on panels is #c9cad1 or brighter.
5. Pills: every status badge is a round pill tinted green, amber or red with its word; a plain badge is neutral.
6. Buttons: the main action (the card action, the form submit, Send, Report a problem, Open) is a white pill with
   near-black text. Other buttons are quiet glass pills. Status buttons (Found it, Solved, Approve, Not this, Not
   solved, Decline) are tinted pills in their colour, with their word. Safety buttons are red-tinted pills.
7. Safety: a red card is the panel with a soft red glow in its top-left corner; its words keep their colours.
8. Pip: web/app/components/PipAvatar.tsx draws Bolt Pip (glass robot head, glowing cyan eyes, small yellow hard
   hat, side bolts). Every fill is a --pip-* token; the sample's two gradients become flat token fills and the
   eye glow a soft halo, so no SVG ids are needed when several Pips share a page.
9. Readability: every status pair 4.5:1 or better on its own background (also under the red glow and the violet
   light), 44 px touch targets (the seat switcher links go from 40 and 36 px to 44), no sideways scroll at 390 px.
10. Spec S23 in docs/plan/BUILD-SPEC.md is rewritten to "Look: dark glass" with the new tokens.
