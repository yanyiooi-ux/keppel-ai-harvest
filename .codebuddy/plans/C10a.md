## Items, each failing test first

1. **web/lib/how.ts: whyStory(tickets, buildings, trends)**, pure. The Level 23 run of calls (the same runOfCalls the
   meter uses), each call with its date ("15 Sep"), id, where and when from the intake, the complaint, the fix note
   word for word or "still open", and whether the spot called again within 30 days (with the next call's id and
   date). Totals: calls, days first to last call, calls closed with a dial turn, still open, "never fixed" (no
   closed call stayed fixed). The two patterns (too cold, core, mornings; too hot, west, afternoons) grouped from the
   tickets. Fit-out and lease months from buildings.json, the core trend line from trends.json. Every date and
   number from the data, nothing typed in. A test changes a date in a temp copy of tickets.json and the story and the
   rendered section change.
2. **howView(state, sources)**: the story, the meter from the one memory (impactFor on the same calls the AOM seat
   uses: tickets plus the runtime jobs, equal to callsForState), the brain (live version, causes and cases counted
   from the data), the 5 seats (label, what the seat is for, what it approves read from ACTION_RULES, the Pip never
   line), the approvals A1 to A4 (A1's line from the memory), the static S22 "who passes what to whom" list, the HOW
   lines, the 4 ranking steps, the 10 safety stops read from the live pill (id and trigger, 0 AI calls), the AI's
   part in a new solution in one line, the brief's 8 questions word for word with a one-line answer each, the
   role-played line, the demo steps (which seat, which button, the labels read from the code and data: cause labels,
   self-check answers, part titles, charge choices) and the close line word for word.
3. **The story on screen** (web/app/components/how/HowStory.tsx, no hooks, server rendered): WHY (headline, the two
   patterns, the 5 calls, the cause line from the data, the impact meter as a Before and With Pill Desk table with
   its label), WHAT (the 5 seats around Pip and the brain, Building B as a card, the approvals table, the passes
   list), HOW (code decides, the AI words, people approve; the 30-day rule; the 4 steps; safety stops; the AI line;
   the 8 questions; the role-played line).
4. **The live wall** (web/app/components/how/HowLive.tsx, client): the 5 real SeatChats as columns, AOM Tower A
   first, then tenant, technician, senior manager, HQ, each server-rendered from seatView on the one memory and then
   polling /api/state every 2 seconds like its own seat page; plus the Building B card, the same view the HQ seat
   shows (buildingBView), polling /api/state?seat=hq so it changes within one poll after HQ approves. A sticky bar
   with the real Reset (POST /api/reset, then reload, like the top bar) and the demo steps as text. No timer, no
   auto-play. HqDesk's Building B view is exported for reuse (no change in what it draws).
5. **Phone, 390 px**: the chats stack in one column, the bar stays reachable (sticky), no sideways scroll.
6. **Footer**: the close line word for word.
