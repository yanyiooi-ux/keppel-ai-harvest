Pill Desk · how it works

Fix it faster, so the same spot stops calling

Pill Desk gives 5 people their own chat with Pip, all on one brain: the signed rules, a list of likely causes and past cases. Code ranks and routes. The AI only puts things into plain words. People approve only 4 kinds of decision, and safety is a stop, not a vote.

2 · What

5 seats around one brain

Each person gets their own chat with Pip. Under every chat sits one brain: the live pill version (TS-CC, repeat comfort complaints), the cause list and the case library. Building B is a card, not a seat. Tap a seat to open its chat alone.

One brainpill, causes, cases

    Who approves what

    WhatWho
    A1Version 1 of the brainTwo senior experts, already signed
    A2A new solutionHQ

    No approval: self-check, checklist, ticks, known fixes, saving cases.

    Safety is a stop, not an approval. Smoke, water near electrics, someone unwell, ceiling work without a permit: Pip says STOP and who to call, in the rulebook's own words, with no AI. Typed safety words show the stop and alert people; only a button makes the floor wait.

    Who passes what to whom

    1. 1Tenant to technician: visit booked, with the self-check answers
    2. 2Technician to senior manager: not solved, with everything attached
    3. 3Senior manager to HQ: a new solution, with its tests
    4. 4HQ to every building: the approved version
    5. 5AOM to tenant: the charge decision

    Code also routes notices: speak first and low confidence to the senior manager, and every red stop alert to the senior manager and the AOM.

    Check, rank, solve or send up, approve, share

    The AI never ranks. It may add a line of wording under a reason, and the code reason always stays.

    Ranking, 4 code steps

    1. Drop unsafe, not-allowed and warranty-covered fixes.
    2. Match facts against 12 made-up past cases. Only 30-day wins count; failures count against.
    3. Close scores: quicker check first.
    4. Show every reason. Under 3 cases: "low confidence", senior manager alerted.

    The AI only words the reasons. Switch it off and every card, rank and number stays the same.

    Who does what

    • Code decides every rank, state, lock, number and hand-off.
    • The AI (Tencent Hunyuan) words reasons, drafts tenant replies from approved tips, and drafts a new rule's text from the senior manager's typed fix. It also suggests when the rule applies, picked only from a fixed code menu. The senior manager confirms each part; a person confirms anything that counts.
    • People approve only at A1 to A4. Typing never changes anything; only buttons do. Typed safety words show the stop and alert people, but only a button raises it.

    30-day rule

    A fix wins only after 30 days with no repeat at that spot. A repeat costs it points. Nobody confirms their own fix.

    Place tags

    Every job carries building, floor, zone and equipment. The same spot twice in 30 days switches "quick fix" to "find the cause".

    Speak first

    That repeat check alerts the senior manager before the tenant calls again.
